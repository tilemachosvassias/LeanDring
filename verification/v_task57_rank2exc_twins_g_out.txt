          they are NOT iota values
======================================================================
   m(G2(3)) = 351 (U3(3):2) and m(G2(4)) = 416 (J2) are SMALLER than
   the parabolic index (q^6-1)/(q-1) = 364 resp. 1365.  But a
   subgroup N = N_S(A) with 1 <> A abelian has a nontrivial ABELIAN
   NORMAL subgroup, so N cannot be (almost) simple.  Check:
