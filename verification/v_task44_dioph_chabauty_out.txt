CONCLUSION for row 3
==============================================================================
Every member tested is a smooth plane quartic of genus 3 whose Jacobian
has an IRREDUCIBLE degree-6 Frobenius polynomial, not a polynomial in
T^k, at some good prime -- hence J_c is ABSOLUTELY SIMPLE.  (A reducible
Weil polynomial at one prime is no evidence to the contrary; ONE
irreducible prime suffices, and every c tested has one.)  Consequences:
 * no elliptic (or abelian-surface) quotient exists, so elliptic-curve
   Chabauty and quotient-Chabauty are unavailable;
 * Chabauty-Coleman itself needs rank J_c(Q) < 3 -- and no algorithm
   computes rank J_c(Q) for a plane quartic;
 * the family is infinite in l* (triage PART C), so even a per-curve
   miracle would not close the row.
Row 3 is therefore NOT reachable by any presently effective Diophantine
method; the same holds for row 2 for the infinite-family reason alone
(although each individual row-2 curve IS decidable -- see triage PART B).
