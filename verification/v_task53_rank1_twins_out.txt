## U3: |S| = q^3 (q-1)(q+1)^2 (q^2-q+1)/e, e = gcd(3,q+1).
##     gcd(q-1,q+1) | 2, gcd(q-1,q^2-q+1) = 1, gcd(q+1,q^2-q+1) | 3,
##     and the 3-part of q^2-q+1 is exactly 3 when 3 | q+1 (and is
##     cancelled by e).  So for r odd, r != p, r != 3 the r-part
##     lies in a single block and is <= (q+1)^2.  For r = 3 with
##     3 | q+1 it is |q+1|_3^2 <= (q+1)^2.  For r = 2 (q odd)
##     it is |q-1|_2 |q+1|_2^2 with min(|q-1|_2,|q+1|_2) = 2, hence
##     <= 2(q+1)^2.  The p-part is q^3.

##     gcd(q-1, q^2+1) = gcd(q-1,2) = 1, so the three blocks are
##     PAIRWISE COPRIME and ppmax <= max(q^2, q-1, q^2+1) = q^2+1.

##     3 does not divide it since 3 | q).  min(|q-1|_2,|q+1|_2) = 2,
##     so the 2-part is <= 2(q+1); the p-part is q^3; every odd
